Formula definitions and interpretation
Patients reached

Lower of eligible annual patients and planned annual service capacity, multiplied by the selected horizon.

Net economic value

System cost avoided + household cost avoided + productivity gain − variable local treatment cost − annual fixed operating cost − capital cost.

Investment per patient

Capital, fixed operating and variable local treatment costs divided by patients reached over the horizon.

Break-even

Capital cost divided by annual net benefit before capital. No break-even is shown when annual benefit is zero or negative.

These are scenario calculations, not causal estimates. Results depend entirely on supplied assumptions and should be accompanied by sources, sensitivity ranges and clinical review before policy use.
